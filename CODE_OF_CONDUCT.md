# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

V.I.S.O.R. is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Respect privacy, consent, and reasonable project boundaries.
- Keep technical disagreements focused on ideas and evidence.
- Help make participation safe for newcomers and maintainers alike.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's
private information without permission are not acceptable. This applies in
project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.

## Reporting

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue. Use the repository's private
[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/dibun75/visor/security/advisories/new)
form and begin the title with `[Conduct]`. The repository owner receives these
reports privately. Include links or screenshots where useful, and remove
credentials or unrelated personal data.

Reports will be acknowledged within seven days. Information will be shared
only with people needed to review and resolve the incident, except where
disclosure is required by law or necessary to prevent immediate harm.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily suspend,
or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this policy. Decisions
will consider the context, severity, history, and impact of the behavior.
Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is itself a violation.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, release
channels, community events, and other spaces managed by the V.I.S.O.R. project.
